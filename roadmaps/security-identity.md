## Identity Fundamentals
The foundation everything else builds on.
- Authentication vs Authorization: Knowing who, then knowing what.
- OAuth 2.0 / OIDC: Modern delegated identity flows.

## Access Control
Enforcing least privilege in practice.
- RBAC & ABAC: Role and attribute-based access.
- Managed Identities: Removing secrets from application code.

## Network Security
Keeping traffic where it should be.
- Private Endpoints: Removing public exposure of services.
- Zero Trust Networking: Never trust, always verify.

## Secrets & Keys
Handling the things that must never leak.
- Key Vault / Secret Managers: Centralized, audited secret storage.
- Rotation & Expiry: Reducing blast radius over time.

## Threat Detection
Knowing when something's wrong.
- SIEM & Alerting: Correlating signals across systems.
- Anomaly Detection: Spotting the unusual, not just the known-bad.

## Compliance & Governance
Proving the controls actually work.
- Policy as Code: Enforcing guardrails automatically.
- Audit Logging: A defensible trail of who did what.

## Security Architecture
Designing systems that are secure by default.
- Defense in Depth: Layered controls, no single point of failure.
- Incident Response: Preparing for the day something gets through.
