## Compute & Networking
Core building blocks of any cloud environment.
- VMs & Containers: Compute fundamentals across providers.
- VNets & Private Endpoints: Network isolation and secure connectivity.

## Identity & Access
Who can do what, and how it's proven.
- RBAC & Managed Identity: Least-privilege access without secrets.
- OAuth / OBO: Delegated access across services.

## Infrastructure as Code
Repeatable, reviewable infrastructure.
- Terraform: State, modules, workspaces.
- Bicep: Native Azure IaC and what-if deployments.

## CI/CD
Shipping infrastructure and applications safely.
- Pipelines: Build, test, approve, deploy.
- GitOps: Declarative deployment from a single source of truth.

## Observability
Knowing what's actually happening in production.
- Metrics & Logs: Golden signals and structured logging.
- Distributed Tracing: Following a request across services.

## Landing Zones
Enterprise-scale governance and guardrails.
- Governance & Policy: Guardrails at scale.
- Hub-Spoke Networking: Centralized connectivity patterns.

## Platform Engineering
Building the platform other teams build on.
- Internal Developer Platform: Self-service infrastructure.
- Cost & FinOps: Making cloud spend visible and accountable.
